CMMC Level 1 Self-Assessment

An unofficial tool to help you assess your compliance with the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) Level 1 controls.

This self-assessment tool is designed to guide you through the 17 controls of CMMC Level 1. For each control, you can mark your status as "MET," "NOT MET," or "N/A" and provide evidence or comments.

Once you have completed the assessment, you can download a PDF summary of your results, including a signature block to affirm the accuracy of your responses.

Disclaimer: This is a free, unofficial tool. No data is stored on a server. All responses are saved locally in your browser only.
